**Built**
- `supabase/demo-seed.sql` (generated by `pnpm db:demo` from `scripts/demo/content.ts`): 32 fictional articles (26 AR, 6 FR) across every section and genre, 3 demo authors who cannot log in, 5 demo tags, 6 credited photos; one breaking, one sponsored, one correction, one interview, three opinion/column pieces, 25+-word headlines, a headline mixing Arabic, a Latin club name and a score, an AR↔FR linked translation, an article with 10 paragraphs (in-article ad positions), an article in a secondary section. `demo-clear.sql` removes all of it (tested).
